# Merchant workspace and table ordering

Mode: Operate. This extends the established Makanlah visual world and existing merchant/menu/QR features. The user asked for the merchant QR system to follow familiar ordering workflows, then explicitly approved direct website implementation while preserving the page aesthetics. Figma construction is deferred.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Give staff direct access to orders, menu items, tables, sales and settings. Remove promotional headings and explanatory panels that do not help staff complete a task. The user's 27 October correction requires operational screens and ASD-STE100-guided text.

OWN-WORLD: Preserve cream #FEF3E2, gold #F4AE52, burnt orange #D4621A and brown #2A1A0E, Bricolage Grotesque headings, DM Sans controls, restrained outlines and rounded food photography. Dense operational content uses the existing warm palette without introducing a separate brand.

STORY: The merchant sees new orders, accepts and prepares them, marks food ready and served, and records payment collected at the counter. Guests scan a table code, customise dishes, place an order without an account and follow its receipt.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact left navigation and a business/status bar frame three order lanes with independent scrolling. A short page title, update time and filters precede the orders. Each ticket shows its table, customer, elapsed time, items, notes and next action. Phones start with a single-column order list and direct fulfilment controls. Staff can select board view.

FORM: Direct correction of the existing application. Preserve the approved palette and typography. Use consistent product terms, short instructions and functional state labels. Normal email/password sign-in replaces sample role controls. QR codes use the current website address, without a developer destination field. See INTERFACE-LANGUAGE.md.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Boundaries

All new data uses the existing local database and media adapters. Changing configured storage destinations remains supported. Pay-at-counter records are operational records; no card charging, banking, payment processor or POS hardware is connected. Sample orders are excluded from queues and sales totals. Example restaurants retain their existing sample disclosures. Existing public discovery and social pages keep their compositions.
