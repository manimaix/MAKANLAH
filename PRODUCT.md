# Makanlah

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product purpose
Help people discover Malaysian food, browse nearby places, find food for their tastes, share photos and videos, and save food experiences. Developed by Nextgen Tech to bring Malaysian food to everyone.

## Current scope
Implement the approved public Figma website as a responsive working local application. Include discovery, recommendations, Leaflet/OpenStreetMap, optional device location with manual city fallback, a vertical video/photo feed, community posts, drafts, local image/video uploads, likes, comments, reviews, saved places, profiles, preferences and About Us. Three server-enforced roles: user, merchant and admin. Merchants manage listings, menus, QR tables and orders. Admins approve listings and moderate content and users.

## Brand commitments
Preserve Figma's cream #FEF3E2, gold #F4AE52, orange #D4621A and brown #2A1A0E, Bricolage Grotesque headings, DM Sans body, food photography, 1440px desktop and 390px mobile composition. Adapt fixed Figma positions into flowing, responsive layout.

## Persistence and delivery
SQLite and filesystem uploads locally; configurable PostgreSQL and S3-compatible storage destinations online. No cloud publication requested in this turn. Seeded listings, reviews and prices are labelled examples. Community credibility and paid partnerships are distinct; never claim to prove a review is honest. QR orders use pay at counter; online payment is outside the requested integration.
